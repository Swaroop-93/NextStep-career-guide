ABOUT NEXTSTEP

Your Career Is A Journey, Not A Single Decision.

NextStep is designed to reduce career confusion by connecting a student's current education, interests and goals to a practical step-by-step plan.

Instead of simply telling students about careers, NextStep helps them understand what they can do next, what skills they need, what they should learn, what projects they can build, and where that journey can lead.
